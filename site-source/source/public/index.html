The Clarity analysis standard

Every bill gets one unavoidable question: Who benefits?

AI will explain the bill in plain English, but it will not hand down a political verdict. It will separate the text, outside evidence, and reasoned analysis—and cite the source behind every material claim.

REQUIRED ON EVERY BILL Who benefits, who pays, and who carries the risk?
AI BILL EXPLAINER AVAILABLE ON SOURCE-BACKED BILLS
01
What actually changes?

Current law, proposed language, amendments, dates, agency discretion, and sunset provisions.

02
Who benefits?

Direct beneficiaries first, then likely secondary beneficiaries—named as specifically as the evidence allows.

03
Who pays or carries risk?

Taxpayers, affected communities, regulated entities, workers, consumers, states, and future budgets.

04
Who is backing or opposing it?

Sponsors, committees, disclosed donors, outside spending, lobby filings, and public organizational positions.

05
What could be challenged?

Potential legal issues are labeled analysis. Actual lawsuits appear only when filed and link to the docket and rulings.

06
What remains uncertain?

Missing data, disputed estimates, implementation choices, assumptions, and confidence labels.

AI explains. Sources prove.No unsupported claims. No guilt by graph.

Receipts, not rumors

Every claim should lead back to the record.

Clarity for US will preserve the original source link, retrieval date, coverage limits, and any transformation applied. Plain-English summaries explain records; they never replace them.

01

Source firstLink to the filing, bill text, executive order, roll call, docket, or opinion.

02

Show uncertaintyLabel missing, delayed, disputed, and estimated information.

03

No guilt by graphConnections show disclosed relationships—not proof of wrongdoing.

04

Fact versus analysisAI labels what the text says, what outside evidence reports, and what the system infers.
